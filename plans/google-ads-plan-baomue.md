## 1. CAMPAIGN STRUCTURE & BUDGET ALLOCATION

### Architecture Overview

With a 30,000 THB/month budget, we run a lean 4-campaign structure. Every campaign has a distinct role -- brand defense, high-intent capture, mid-funnel service awareness, and remarketing/discovery.

```
Account: BAOMUE Dental Clinic
|
|-- Campaign 1: [Search] Brand Defense
|   Budget: 3,000 THB/mo (10%)
|   Bidding: Maximize Clicks (cap 15 THB)
|   Purpose: Capture branded searches, protect against competitors
|
|-- Campaign 2: [Search] High-Value Services
|   Budget: 15,000 THB/mo (50%)
|   Bidding: Maximize Conversions (transition to tCPA after 30 conversions)
|   Purpose: จัดฟัน, Invisalign, รากฟันเทียม, วีเนียร์
|
|-- Campaign 3: [Search] Entry-Level + Symptom-Based
|   Budget: 7,000 THB/mo (23%)
|   Bidding: Maximize Conversions
|   Purpose: ผ่าฟันคุด, ฟอกสีฟัน, ปวดฟัน, ขูดหินปูน -- patient acquisition
|
|-- Campaign 4: [Performance Max] Full-Funnel Discovery
|   Budget: 5,000 THB/mo (17%)
|   Bidding: Maximize Conversion Value
|   Purpose: Display/YouTube/Discovery/Maps remarketing + prospecting
```

### Budget Rationale

| Campaign | Monthly Budget | Daily Budget | Rationale |
|----------|---------------|-------------|-----------|
| Brand Defense | 3,000 THB | 100 THB | Low volume but must own 95%+ impression share on brand terms |
| High-Value Services | 15,000 THB | 500 THB | Highest LTV services (Invisalign 39K+, implants 45K+) justify premium CPC |
| Entry-Level + Symptoms | 7,000 THB | 233 THB | Patient acquisition funnel -- lower ticket but builds database for upsell |
| Performance Max | 5,000 THB | 167 THB | Asset-based campaigns for Maps visibility + YouTube/Display remarketing |

### Estimated CPC & Volume (Bangkok dental market)

Thai dental CPCs in Bangkok typically range 8-25 THB for general terms, 25-60 THB for high-competition terms (จัดฟันใส, Invisalign). At 30,000 THB/month:
- Estimated clicks/month: 1,200-1,800
- Estimated conversions (at 5-8% CVR): 60-144
- Target CPA: 200-500 THB per lead

### Bidding Strategy Transition Plan

| Phase | Timeframe | Strategy | Trigger |
|-------|-----------|----------|---------|
| Phase 1 | Week 1-3 | Maximize Clicks (Search), Maximize Conversions (PMax) | Launch -- gathering data |
| Phase 2 | Week 4-6 | Maximize Conversions | 15+ conversions accumulated |
| Phase 3 | Week 7+ | Target CPA (300 THB) | 30+ conversions in 30 days |
| Phase 4 | Month 3+ | Target ROAS (if value tracking enabled) | Stable conversion data |
